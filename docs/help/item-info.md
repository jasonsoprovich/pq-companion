# Item Info Overlay

**Prerequisite:** Zeal 1.5 (the [v1.5.0-alpha0](https://github.com/CoastalRedwood/Zeal/releases/tag/v1.5.0-alpha0) test build, or the official 1.5 release once it's out).

Open an item's display window in game and the Item Info overlay shows that item: key facts picked by item type, its effects, which of your characters hold it, its gear-upgrade score, tradeskill uses grouped by discipline, quests, vendors, drops and other sources. Sources in your current zone are listed first, long lists collapse to a count you can expand, and the header has an open-in-app link.

### Where to find it

The overlay stays out of the Overlays list and Settings until Zeal reports your first item, then appears like any other dashboard panel with a pop-out.

### Customizing

The **Item Info** overlay's settings in the **Overlays** hub have a live preview and controls for each section and row, separately for the dashboard and the pop-out: show or hide, drag to reorder, list limits, and whether an empty section says so or hides. You can also have the pop-out open by itself when you click an item, without taking focus, and hide again after a delay.
