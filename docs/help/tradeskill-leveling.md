# Tradeskill Leveling & Recipes

**Prerequisite:** Zeal-exported tradeskill values for the Tradeskills tab; no Zeal export needed for the recipe browser or planner themselves.

The Recipes browser lets you look up any tradeskill combine, pin favourites, and see every recipe an item is used in from that item's own Tradeskills tab. The leveling planner builds on top of it: pick a tradeskill and a target skill, and it produces a staged, step-by-step plan using only recipes you can actually make.

### Fastest vs. cheapest

Choose "fastest" for the fewest total combines, or "cheapest" for the least plat spent — the planner factors in the cost of any sub-combines you'd need to craft along the way, and warns you when a step crosses into a different tradeskill. Paths are derived entirely from the game database, so they stay accurate as recipes and trivials change.

### Success chances & skill-ups

Every recipe view shows your live success and failure percentage from your actual skill and crafting-stat gear, including the trivial breakpoint where you can no longer fail and a skill-up chance estimate. Jewelcraft, Alchemy, and Poison Mastery AAs and Maelin Starpyre's skill-up buff are applied automatically. Race-locked cultural recipes are flagged in both the browser and the planner so you're never steered into a combine your character's race can't perform.

### Inventory status

Both the Recipes browser and the leveling planner flag each component of a combine as either already in your inventory or still missing, so you can see at a glance what you need to go gather before starting a crafting run.
