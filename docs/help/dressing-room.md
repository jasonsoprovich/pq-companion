# Dressing Room

See your character's model, rendered from your own EverQuest client files,
wearing the gear from their last Zeal inventory export.

**Prerequisites:** your EverQuest folder set in Settings, and a Zeal
inventory export for full gear.

- **Try on** any item in a slot to preview it in place of what's equipped;
  the reset button returns a slot to your real gear.
- **Looks** — the log records neither gender nor face, so pick gender,
  face, hair and eyes here. They're saved per character.
- **Other races** — preview your gear on another race (a race change or an
  illusion), with its own gender and looks that aren't saved.
