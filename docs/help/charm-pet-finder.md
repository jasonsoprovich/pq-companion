# Charm Pet Finder

For the charm classes, the difference between a good pet and a great one is real DPS. Charm Pet Finder lists every charmable NPC in a zone for your class and charm spell, ranked by damage output, so you can walk into a camp already knowing which mob is worth charming.

### DPS ranking & land chance

Candidates are ranked by estimated damage (derived from the mob's attack values and delay), and each carries the land chance for your charm against it — computed with the same resist engine as the [Resist Calculator](help:resist-calc). A target above your charm spell's level cap is flagged with a warning, since no amount of resist luck will hold it.

### Planning ahead

You can select a charm spell you can't cast yet to see what a future pet pool looks like, so the page doubles as a leveling plan for where to camp once the next charm comes online. Charm spells are gated by their real Quarm class levels rather than a curated guess. Magicians are included too, with Call of the Arch Mage, the level 65 charm for summoned creatures.

### Charisma

For enchanters, charisma nudges the charm land chance, so the Charisma field defaults to your active character's real value — base stat plus worn gear and AAs — rather than a generic number. It stays editable if you want to test a different value, and a reset button next to it returns to your character's.

The same ranking appears in the [Pet Overlay](help:pet-overlay) whenever you don't have a pet.
